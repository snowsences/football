How the rating works

The overall rating gives 75% weight to drama and 25% to action. Drama considers how competitive the game stays, uncertainty, and meaningful shifts in advantage. Action considers the rate of big plays, productive plays, and scoring drives.

A rating is a recommendation, not a guarantee. Live ratings are provisional. Games without enough play-by-play data stay unrated. Basic ratings use the score progression when win probabilities are unavailable.

Data comes from ESPN’s unofficial feeds. Availability and update timing can vary. Scores and stats stay hidden until you reveal them or mark a game watched. Headlines and game photos are not displayed. Team records appear for revealed games.
